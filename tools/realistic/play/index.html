Drive a tram through real Riga

Streamed photogrammetric reality mesh · BEDRE tram simulation
Private local experiment. The 3D imagery is the Rīgas valstspilsētas pašvaldība (Riga City Council) 3D reality model (GeoRīga). It is fetched from the city's public services into a local cache on this computer, is not licensed for redistribution, and must not be published, deployed or shared.

Quality: Chrome or Safari on Apple Silicon recommended.

Generated from `riga.json` (`t:"rel"`, names matching baznīca / katedrāle / sinagoga / draudze / lūgšanu / Habad ..., OSM `building=church|cathedral|chapel`, plus mis-typed ones found by OSM centroid-in-footprint matching: Sv. Review shots: `scripts/out/church-before-after-<slug>.png`, `church-sheet.png`, `church-skyline-{day,evening}.png`.

Not in the data at all: Grebenščikova old-believer prayer house (Gogoļa iela; no cadastre name, no OSM name, no landmark entry), Sv. Franciska, Sv. Pāvila (Ķīpsala), Sv. Alberta lies in Āgenskalns (present), the Lutheran Trīsvienības on Sarkandaugava is outside every map. They cannot be curated without a footprint id.

## Why churches looked generic (findings)

1. **No hero entry = ordinary building.** Only 11 of the ~45 religious buildings had a hero entry. All others went through the plain plane-fitter with `style 0` (apartment windows), a RANDOM roof colour from the neighbourhood tile/tin palette (orange, teal, grey patchwork), neutral walls.
2. **Even heroes had no church window style** beyond the narrow `style 1`; no arched windows, no light plaster vs brick distinction.
3. **Mis-typed**: Sv. Pētera un Pāvila (Citadeles iela 7) is `pub` -> flat extrusion with the generic procedural cone spire.
4. **No height grid (outside the reality-mesh coverage)**: Mežaparks Kunga Apskaidrošanās, Kristus Karaļa, Meža prospekts 11A, Nesaules iela 7; Purvciems Svētās Trīsvienības, Labā Vēsts (4351 m2), Jehovas, Jaunapustuliskā. They were flat boxes with a black procedural cone (`addSpire`).
5. **Plane fitter noise**: towers fused with nave roofs (Nevsky: brown slab + lump), crumpled roofs on halls (synagogue), spire lost on small towers, round lumps where apses are.
6. Several `rel` entries are not churches in form (halls, a block with a church inside, parish houses): left as ordinary buildings, listed below as "no".

Rendering paths before: **planar** = plane-fitted mesh (all with a grid, TIN only as fallback for < 20 m2 footprints), **flat** = flat extrusion + procedural spire (no grid). After: **kit** = `curated-churches.mjs` on the measured grid, **kit/synth** = kit on a grid synthesised from the footprint.

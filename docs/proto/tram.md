# Tramvaja vadītājs — the tram-driver game

Live path: `/tramvajs/` (second HTML entry `tramvajs/index.html` of the Vite build; no backend, no score calls). A small 3D tram-driving game on the real tracks of Riga (the BEDRE — Rīga
world: real map, shapes, trees): drive a tram along real routes, stop with the front door on the yellow marker at the real stops, obey signals and the timetable, share the line with other
trams, cars and people. No operator logos; blue-and-white low-floor tram, plain route boards. The earlier prototype "Kur es esmu?" (`/kur/`) was removed (see the end).

## Routes (4) and what they correspond to

Generated by `node src/proto/tram/tools/build-route.mjs` from `public/data[/<map>]/riga.json` (`tramways`, `stops`) plus the OSM stop **names** in the read-only cache `tools/.cache/osm_transit*.json`.
**Directed track graph.** Every OSM tram way is `oneway=yes` (one service spur excepted) and `riga.json` keeps each way's vertices in the way's own direction, so a way is a directed edge chain: a
route can only ever travel *along* ways (each direction of a street is its own track), never reverse, and branches only at nodes where ways really join (facing switches: 60 on the centre map).
The centre graph has 1274 nodes; its directed core (717 nodes) is strongly connected, the rest are entries / exits at the map edge. A stop in `riga.json` is the OSM stop position *on the track*
(one per direction); platforms are drawn on the right of the direction of travel (the OSM platform nodes are too sparse to decide per stop; where one exists it is on the right: 12 of 13 checked).
A centre route = ordered stop **names**; the generator joins them with directed shortest paths, lists *every* stop the path passes and fails if that is not exactly the expected sequence.

Notes. (1) The first draft's U-turn at the Opera is gone because the graph is directed: the Opera platforms of the two directions are different nodes on different ways. (2) `centrs-2` starts at
Ģertrūdes iela, not at Brīvības iela: the OSM track between Bērnu pasaule and Ģertrūdes iela is a turning loop of R ≈ 3 m (a hairpin no tram — and no body of 25 m — could follow), so the route
begins after it; `centrs-1` ends at Bērnu pasaule (the end of its track, before the loop). (3) Brīvības / Elizabetes are not tram streets in the data; nothing was invented. (4) Each route also carries
an `ai` entry: the **opposite direction** (own track) that the oncoming AI trams run.

**Validation** (`src/proto/tram/validate.js`, run by the generator and by `smoke:tram`): continuity (no gap > 400 m between vertices, no duplicate points), no reversing over the own track, no U-turn
(< 150° in 40 m), heading change ≤ 35° per 10 m except at real corners of the OSM data (tight spots are listed, a spline artefact fails), a 2.52 m body module would sag ≤ 0.15 m everywhere,
every stop ≤ 3.5 m from the track, strictly in order, ≥ 150 m apart. Results: all 4 routes + 3 opposite tracks pass (max 41°/10 m, min R 19 m, module sag ≤ 0.13 m).
`track.js` relaxes the spline where the local radius is below 7.2 m (points move ≤ 2.5 m, so a stop stays beside its platform).

## How to run

* `npm run dev` → http://localhost:5173/tramvajs/ (`?route=<id>`, `?run=short|full`, `?lang=lv|en`, `?quality=low|medium|high`, `?autostart=1`). A route on another map reloads the page (the world of that map loads).
* Build: `npm run build:web` (or the smoke script's own `scripts/.dist-tram`), `npm run preview`, open `/tramvajs/`.
* Controls: W/↑ power, S/↓ brake, space emergency brake, B bell, E/Enter doors, C cab view, M mute, Esc pause. Phones: drag the lever (springs back), Durvis / Zvans / Kabīne, ♪ mute, pause; settings: sound, lever on the left, cab view, time of day.
* Test: `npm run smoke:tram` (builds, previews on 5193; `GPU=1` real GPU, `ONLY=chromium-desktop,chromium-pixel5,webkit-iphone13`, `SKIP_BUILD=1`, `FAST=1` skips the 3×-full-run deadlock check).

### Timetable (task item 4)
`makeRun()` runs the **same physics** with a good-but-human reference driver (95.5 % of the ideal speed profile, comfortable braking, normal door cycles) and records its arrival times (`due`) and door
closing times (+3 s: the departure bell and a human's slack → `depart`; **the doors cannot close before it, so early departure is impossible**). The perfect autopilot (100 % pace; it eases off up to
14 % when well ahead and hurries up to +10 % when late) arrives −2…+2 s of `due` on an empty line. **Delays caused by the surroundings are credited** — a red / yellow signal, a person at a crossing
or a tram ahead: the time lost against what a free line would have allowed (×0.9) is added to the due times, but *only while the tram really is behind the timetable* (so a credit can never make an
arrival look early) — so holding at red costs nothing. In the live game with all hazards on (traffic, people, signals, AI trams) the scripted driver's arrival error was **−2.0 … +1.8 s in the final smoke run (single runs of every route, short + full), ≤ 1.8 s in 12 repeated full runs**, and the smoke test asserts |error| ≤ 5 s for every stop of every route / run on desktop. HUD: a calm pill "Agrāk 12 s / Laikā / Vēlāk 14 s" (position against the timetable, standing
time at a stop keeps the arrival error); the stop toast shows the signed error.

### Scoring and the report card
Per stop 100 points: accuracy 40 (front-door error ≤ 0.5 m perfect …), timekeeping 30 (±8 s free), smoothness 20 (jerk, hard braking, curve overspeed, the jolt at standstill — all scaled by the load aboard),
speed 10; penalties per run: red signal −15, collision-protection stop −10 (−6 for a person), −12 reserved. Report card: stars (0-3) for **precision, punctuality, comfort (stop smoothness + the live comfort meter),
rules & safety (red signals, protection stops, overspeed, missed stops)**, total stars, **passengers happy %**, penalties, personal best per route + run in `localStorage` (`tramvajs.pb.<route>.<run>`, try/catch),
a share text (Web Share / clipboard). Reckless driving (full power, emergency braking, ignores signals) scores ≈ 30-45 % of the perfect run and gets 1 star.

## Performance (task item 5)

What changed: `World.setViewDistance(d)` is called every frame with a street-level value (1000 m on phones = cheap tree crowns + thinned tiny props; 640 m on desktop); far plane 1100 m, fog 260 → 900 m (phones 170 → 620 m);
sun shadow window 90 m (phones) / 150 m; the close-up ground layer gets a focus that jumps in 42 m steps 38 m ahead of the tram (the engine repaints a 110 m window whenever its focus moved 22 m: one repaint per 3 s
instead of per 1.6 s) and is switched off on phones (no repaint at all); trams / wires / poles are instanced or merged (a tram is ~45 draw calls).

## What was cut / partly done
* **Not done:** a recorded-voice announcement (by design), a real Riga timetable (GTFS), switch *route choice* (routes are fixed paths; the facing switches only slow the tram), left-hand platforms (all platforms are on the right), the full line 11 across 3 maps, level-crossing barriers (signals only), cars *turning onto* the track corridor are handled by the yield rule only (no stop lines painted for cars).
* **Partly:** "other trams on their own timetable" — the lead / follower run the player's reference timetable, the oncoming trams free-run their opposite track with 12-20 s dwells (no published timetable); street life yields only where a vehicle *approaches* the corridor (a vehicle already on it clears it or is removed after 5 s); evening headlights are light cones, not real lights; the winter look has no snow on trees; the engine's ground-detail repaint spike is mitigated, not removed (it needs an incremental repaint in the engine); mirrors are not modelled (not needed).
* Phones: only emulation was available (Chromium Pixel 5 profile with 4× CPU throttle, real WebKit iPhone 13 profile); real devices and real iOS audio interruptions (phone calls, silent switch) were not tested.

## Recommendations for the engine (not done here — engine modules were not touched)
1. `Traffic`: a per-class on/off that survives `Quality._rung` → `traffic.setFraction` (the game wraps the method on the instance), and a public hold / release for a mover (the game sets `m.base = 0`).
2. `DetailLayer.update(x, z, dist)`: gates on `dist < 85` and repaints a fixed 110 m window every 22 m; a street-level game needs a configurable window / look-ahead and an incremental repaint (paint a few rows per frame).
3. `Lighting.update` ties fog to the camera distance (`near = dist·1.9`): expose fog near / far (the game overwrites them every frame).
4. `ThemeManager`: a Game-free mode (or export `build(id)` + `_ground()`); `env.js` copies the three looks and the shader chain.
5. `World`: a constructor option "no hole game" (spawn, placement checks, mover registration), a public `hide(obj)` instead of the private `_hide`, and a way to set instance colours of a kind (the game lightens cars through `mesh.setColorAt`).
6. `PROP_DEFS.tram`: ship an articulated model (this game's `tramModel.js` could replace it); person variants with animation hooks.
7. `vite.config.js` `dataPreload`: a per-page flag instead of the path guard added for `/tramvajs/`.
8. `maps`: an `applyMap(slug)` that does not read the URL / localStorage.
9. Low tier on phones still draws ~1 M triangles: a street-level tree budget would help most.

## The tram game's own city (`src/proto/tram/city/**`, `public/data/tram/**`, `tools/tramcity/**`)

Decision of the owner: the hole game keeps its buildings exactly as they are; the tram game has its own building renderer and its own data and may diverge. The tram game no longer *draws* the hole game's
building mesh (`?city=0` brings the old look back for before / after sheets).

**Hook.** `src/proto/shared/street-world.js` takes an optional `buildCity(ctx)`; the tram game passes `buildTramCity` (`city/index.js`). The engine's `World` still constructs its building batch (no hook exists
to skip it and the hole-game modules are not edited); `buildTramCity` reads that batch **read-only** (positions / normals / colours of every building's geometry), hides it (`buildingMesh.visible = false`)
and draws its own meshes. Cost: the engine's batch is still built (~0.4 s on the Mac) plus ~0.42 s for the tram city. Not done: skipping the engine's build (needs an engine option).

**What the city module does with each building** (5,241 on the centre map)
* roofs, spires, domes, portico columns and any wall piece that is not a long ground-based wall are *passed through* from the engine geometry, roof-coloured faces re-coloured with the sampled roof colour;
* every ground-based wall face (>= 1.5 m, base at 0) is replaced by this module's facade: wall strips with baked ambient occlusion (dark base, soft top), plinth colour, and a **shader window grid**
  (glass with a sky-tinted fresnel term, shop glazing in the ground band, windows lit at night — shops brighter —, snow on every up-facing face) for **all** buildings: `city/material.js`, `city/facade.js`;
* **corridor buildings** (within 60 m of any track of any route of that map: 651 on the centre map, 193 on Mežaparks; list in `public/data/tram/corridor-<map>.json.gz`) get, for every wall within 72 m of the track
  that is not a party wall / courtyard (point-in-polygon test against the neighbouring corridor buildings), **real relief geometry**: window jambs / lintels / sills / mullions on the same grid as the shader windows,
  projecting cornice and string course, plinth, shop piers, signboards (plain coloured boards, no names), awnings, a door, second-floor balconies (slab + balustrade + rail) on ~half the residential buildings,
  dormers on mansard roofs, chimneys. Relief meshes are separate per 150 m chunk and drawn only within 130 m of the camera (95 m on phones): `city.update(camera)` flips `visible`, no allocation.
* colours: curated table (`city/curated.js`, 10 buildings: Opera + annex, National Theatre, four Centrāltirgus pavilions, Latviešu biedrības nams, Music Academy, Stockmann) > sampled colour of
  `tools/sampling` (2,641 walls / 3,444 roofs, 1,249 rhythm records) with a chroma-restoration heuristic (`look.js restore`) > the engine's colour; engine walls that are dark grey without a sampled colour get a
  warm plaster palette picked by id.
* chunks: 294 lo meshes (cast shadows) + hi meshes (no shadows); the whole city is ~1.15 M triangles in the buffers, of which the relief (hi) layer is ~0.59 M but only the chunks near the camera are drawn.

**Data files** (all derived numbers, no pixels; loaded only by the tram game, lazily): `public/data/tram/look-centrs.json.gz` (55.5 KB gz), `corridor-centrs.json.gz` (1.7 KB), `corridor-mezaparks.json.gz` (0.5 KB).
Rebuild: `node tools/tramcity/build.mjs` (reads `tools/sampling/data/params-look.json` and the route files). Debug helpers: `node scripts/tramcity-shots.mjs` (viewpoint screenshots, `--city 0` = before),
`node scripts/tramcity-sheet.mjs` (comparison sheets in `scripts/out/tramcity/`), `node tools/tramcity/pick.mjs <s> <px> <py>` (ray-pick a pixel and print the face's attributes).

## Round 2: measured facades (corridor buildings of the centre map)

**What changed.** Facades are no longer a uniform shader grid on corridor walls. `tools/tramcity/measure.mjs` reads the 10 cm/px rectified facade images that `tools/sampling/measure2.html` cut from the cached city reality mesh (intermediates
in `tools/.cache/sampling/rect/`, never shipped; 26.7 k images, 2,199 of them for the 388 corridor buildings that have any) and extracts per facade run: the floor period (autocorrelation of the row profile of "darker than its surroundings" pixels), floor rows
(comb-fitted to the wall, not a uniform guess), window columns at their **real positions** (peaks of the column profile inside each floor band, merged across floors into consensus columns), window widths, the ground-floor band (height, shop glazing columns or plain / rusticated), and the facade's sunlit wall colour.
`tools/tramcity/finalize.mjs` turns that into `public/data/tram/facades-centrs.json.gz` (80 KB gz; numbers only) and applies `tools/tramcity/overrides.json` (hand corrections). The game matches every wall edge of the engine geometry to a run (same direction, within 0.9 m, inside its extent) and emits a real glass quad per window at the
measured position, framed by jambs / lintel / sill (+ mullion), with the cornice, string course above the ground floor, a darker base band, shop glazing between piers with fascia boards (plain colour, no names), awnings on only ~1 in 5 shop bays, one door per facade, balconies on the second floor of some residential
buildings, dormers over the top-floor windows of mansard roofs (`city/facade.js emitFromPlan`). Unmeasured corridor walls use a corrected generic plan (windows ~1.25 x 1.9 m on 3.2-4.0 m floors, bays 2.3-3.4 m, windows centred, none within 0.5 m of a corner) and the shader grid for non-corridor walls was clamped to the same proportions.
A fill light (16 % of the diffuse colour as emissive on non-up faces, day only) keeps facades that face away from the sun readable.

**Measured vs generic (centre map, corridor of 651 buildings).** 330 buildings have measured data: 1,246 facade runs, **982 with a measured window grid (20,156 windows, mask = all columns on every floor)**, 1,246 with a measured albedo, 509 with a measured ground band (gh + shop glazing columns). In the game 857 of the 3,188 detailed corridor
edges use a measured grid, 991 get a measured colour, the other 2,331 edges use the corrected generic plan (these include all of Mežaparks: no mesh facades were rectified there). Not measured at all (generic): window head type (all flat; arches / segmental heads are not drawn), cornice / string-course heights and depths (defaults), pilasters, oriels, corner turrets, gables, door positions (first free spot), balcony positions (second floor, every 3rd window),
attic windows (dormers where the roof rises above the eave).

**Hand review.** `scripts/out/tramcity/review/sheet-01..10.png` (100 most visible measured facades of centrs-3, ranked by time in view from the cab: rectified real with the measured rectangles drawn over it | plain render of the parameters | parameters) was looked at one by one. Result: roughly 70 correct or close
(floors and columns line up with the photo), ~30 unusable (smeared / partial / scaffolded / tree-covered rectifications, small side walls): 30 of them are skipped in `overrides.json` (they fall back to the generic plan and lose their measured colour), 2 get a floor-count override (`49616143_3` -> 4, `49615709_2` -> 5). The measurement is weakest on glass and very ornamented facades and on runs shorter than ~6 m. `scripts/out/tramcity/facade-review.png` = 24 facades: rectified real | the game facade (screenshot facing the wall) | parameters; honest verdict: the first 8 were looked at at full size, the other 16 only on the (tall, small) thumbnail sheet, so the verdict for those is weaker. In the 8: the window grid and floor count match the photo on #1, #3, #4, #6, #7, #8 (regular grids with the right number of floors; #6 and #4 render darker / cooler than the photo because the wall faces away from the sun), #5 is poor (two floors detected, merged wide windows), #2 is a smeared rectification (generic plan used). The ground floor is the weakest part everywhere (glazing blocks without the real rusticated / arcaded variety).

**Colour.** Per facade: lit wall pixels (brightest consistent part, not the darkest openings) -> linear -> divided by the bright half of the pavement in front of the facade (the existing illumination reference of the sampling pipeline) x an assumed pavement albedo of 0.32, per-channel cast limited to 0.8-1.25, luminance normalised into 0.34-0.62, then **two heuristic cast corrections** (shaded facades read cool: blue pulled back to neutral; rosy cast: red pulled back) because the raw measurement came out lavender / pink on shaded facades. The chroma-boost heuristic of round 1 is used only where no measurement exists. Colours are **measured in hue direction, normalised in lightness, not true paint colours.** Trim / base / plinth colours are derived from the wall colour (lightened / darkened), not measured.

**Heroes.** The Opera gets a portico (6 octagonal columns, stylobate + steps, entablature, pediment with a sculpture-group hint, grey-green roof slopes) on its longest edge that looks towards the old centre; the four Centrāltirgus pavilions get glazed arch gables with mullions on their two shortest ends. Both were only checked from a few angles (`scripts/out/tramcity/opera-a.png`): the Opera's own wall behind the portico still carries generic windows and from the cab (s 1200-1300) it is **not** unmistakable; Stockmann uses the generic plan.

**Not done in round 2** (frankly): kerbs, cobbles / setts, zebra crossings and markings, parked cars, street trees, boulevard lamps, platform shelters with name signs, traffic lights moved to real junctions, the National Library / Old Town panorama heroes, the Freedom Monument, round / segmental window heads, measured cornices, measured colours for non-corridor buildings, a facade pipeline for Mežaparks / the Āgenskalns-style maps (no rectified images exist there), true sunlit-texel colours from the full-resolution textures (the existing 10 cm rectifications were used; the i3s cache was not re-sampled).

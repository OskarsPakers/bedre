# Tramvaja vadītājs — the tram-driver game

Live path: `/tramvajs/` (second HTML entry `tramvajs/index.html` of the Vite build; no backend, no score calls). A small 3D tram-driving game on the real tracks of Riga (the BEDRE — Rīga
world: real map, shapes, trees): drive a tram along real routes, stop with the front door on the yellow marker at the real stops, obey signals and the timetable, share the line with other
trams, cars and people. No operator logos; blue-and-white low-floor tram, plain route boards. The earlier prototype "Kur es esmu?" (`/kur/`) was removed (see the end).

## Routes (4) and what they correspond to

Generated by `node src/proto/tram/tools/build-route.mjs` from `public/data[/<map>]/riga.json` (`tramways`, `stops`) plus the OSM stop **names** in the read-only cache `tools/.cache/osm_transit*.json`.
**Directed track graph.** Every OSM tram way is `oneway=yes` (one service spur excepted) and `riga.json` keeps each way's vertices in the way's own direction, so a way is a directed edge chain: a
route can only ever travel *along* ways (each direction of a street is its own track), never reverse, and branches only at nodes where ways really join (facing switches: 60 on the centre map).
The centre graph has 1274 nodes; its directed core (717 nodes) is strongly connected, the rest are entries / exits at the map edge. A stop in `riga.json` is the OSM stop position *on the track*
(one per direction); platforms are drawn on the right of the direction of travel (the OSM platform nodes are too sparse to decide per stop; where one exists it is on the right: 12 of 13 checked).
A centre route = ordered stop **names**; the generator joins them with directed shortest paths, lists *every* stop the path passes and fails if that is not exactly the expected sequence.

Notes. (1) The first draft's U-turn at the Opera is gone because the graph is directed: the Opera platforms of the two directions are different nodes on different ways. (2) `centrs-2` starts at
Ģertrūdes iela, not at Brīvības iela: the OSM track between Bērnu pasaule and Ģertrūdes iela is a turning loop of R ≈ 3 m (a hairpin no tram — and no body of 25 m — could follow), so the route
begins after it; `centrs-1` ends at Bērnu pasaule (the end of its track, before the loop). (3) Brīvības / Elizabetes are not tram streets in the data; nothing was invented. (4) Each route also carries
an `ai` entry: the **opposite direction** (own track) that the oncoming AI trams run.

**Validation** (`src/proto/tram/validate.js`, run by the generator and by `smoke:tram`): continuity (no gap > 400 m between vertices, no duplicate points), no reversing over the own track, no U-turn
(< 150° in 40 m), heading change ≤ 35° per 10 m except at real corners of the OSM data (tight spots are listed, a spline artefact fails), a 2.52 m body module would sag ≤ 0.15 m everywhere,
every stop ≤ 3.5 m from the track, strictly in order, ≥ 150 m apart. Results: all 4 routes + 3 opposite tracks pass (max 41°/10 m, min R 19 m, module sag ≤ 0.13 m).
`track.js` relaxes the spline where the local radius is below 7.2 m (points move ≤ 2.5 m, so a stop stays beside its platform).

## How to run

* `npm run dev` → http://localhost:5173/tramvajs/ (`?route=<id>`, `?run=short|full`, `?lang=lv|en`, `?quality=low|medium|high`, `?autostart=1`). A route on another map reloads the page (the world of that map loads).
* Build: `npm run build:web` (or the smoke script's own `scripts/.dist-tram`), `npm run preview`, open `/tramvajs/`.
* Controls: W/↑ power, S/↓ brake, space emergency brake, B bell, E/Enter doors, C cab view, M mute, Esc pause. Phones: drag the lever (springs back), Durvis / Zvans / Kabīne, ♪ mute, pause; settings: sound, lever on the left, cab view, time of day.
* Test: `npm run smoke:tram` (builds, previews on 5193; `GPU=1` real GPU, `ONLY=chromium-desktop,chromium-pixel5,webkit-iphone13`, `SKIP_BUILD=1`, `FAST=1` skips the 3×-full-run deadlock check).

### Timetable (task item 4)
`makeRun()` runs the **same physics** with a good-but-human reference driver (95.5 % of the ideal speed profile, comfortable braking, normal door cycles) and records its arrival times (`due`) and door
closing times (+3 s: the departure bell and a human's slack → `depart`; **the doors cannot close before it, so early departure is impossible**). The perfect autopilot (100 % pace; it eases off up to
14 % when well ahead and hurries up to +10 % when late) arrives −2…+2 s of `due` on an empty line. **Delays caused by the surroundings are credited** — a red / yellow signal, a person at a crossing
or a tram ahead: the time lost against what a free line would have allowed (×0.9) is added to the due times, but *only while the tram really is behind the timetable* (so a credit can never make an
arrival look early) — so holding at red costs nothing. In the live game with all hazards on (traffic, people, signals, AI trams) the scripted driver's arrival error was **−2.0 … +1.8 s in the final smoke run (single runs of every route, short + full), ≤ 1.8 s in 12 repeated full runs**, and the smoke test asserts |error| ≤ 5 s for every stop of every route / run on desktop. HUD: a calm pill "Agrāk 12 s / Laikā / Vēlāk 14 s" (position against the timetable, standing
time at a stop keeps the arrival error); the stop toast shows the signed error.

### Scoring and the report card
Per stop 100 points: accuracy 40 (front-door error ≤ 0.5 m perfect …), timekeeping 30 (±8 s free), smoothness 20 (jerk, hard braking, curve overspeed, the jolt at standstill — all scaled by the load aboard),
speed 10; penalties per run: red signal −15, collision-protection stop −10 (−6 for a person), −12 reserved. Report card: stars (0-3) for **precision, punctuality, comfort (stop smoothness + the live comfort meter),
rules & safety (red signals, protection stops, overspeed, missed stops)**, total stars, **passengers happy %**, penalties, personal best per route + run in `localStorage` (`tramvajs.pb.<route>.<run>`, try/catch),
a share text (Web Share / clipboard). Reckless driving (full power, emergency braking, ignores signals) scores ≈ 30-45 % of the perfect run and gets 1 star.

## Performance (task item 5)

What changed: `World.setViewDistance(d)` is called every frame with a street-level value (1000 m on phones = cheap tree crowns + thinned tiny props; 640 m on desktop); far plane 1100 m, fog 260 → 900 m (phones 170 → 620 m);
sun shadow window 90 m (phones) / 150 m; the close-up ground layer gets a focus that jumps in 42 m steps 38 m ahead of the tram (the engine repaints a 110 m window whenever its focus moved 22 m: one repaint per 3 s
instead of per 1.6 s) and is switched off on phones (no repaint at all); trams / wires / poles are instanced or merged (a tram is ~45 draw calls).

Measured with `GPU=1` (the Mac's real GPU through ANGLE / Metal), real-time frames of the autopilot driving the Mežaparks **full** route (2.5 s warm-up, then 10-14 s), day look. "Before" = the
previous prototype build (same route, old code, `scripts/.tram-frames`-style sampler); "after" = `smoke:tram` (`FRAMES` lines). Pixel 5 = Chromium Pixel 5 profile with **4× CPU throttle**; iPhone 13 = real WebKit
with the iPhone 13 profile (emulated viewport / touch, desktop GPU).

## What was cut / partly done
* **Not done:** a recorded-voice announcement (by design), a real Riga timetable (GTFS), switch *route choice* (routes are fixed paths; the facing switches only slow the tram), left-hand platforms (all platforms are on the right), the full line 11 across 3 maps, level-crossing barriers (signals only), cars *turning onto* the track corridor are handled by the yield rule only (no stop lines painted for cars).
* **Partly:** "other trams on their own timetable" — the lead / follower run the player's reference timetable, the oncoming trams free-run their opposite track with 12-20 s dwells (no published timetable); street life yields only where a vehicle *approaches* the corridor (a vehicle already on it clears it or is removed after 5 s); evening headlights are light cones, not real lights; the winter look has no snow on trees; the engine's ground-detail repaint spike is mitigated, not removed (it needs an incremental repaint in the engine); mirrors are not modelled (not needed).
* Phones: only emulation was available (Chromium Pixel 5 profile with 4× CPU throttle, real WebKit iPhone 13 profile); real devices and real iOS audio interruptions (phone calls, silent switch) were not tested.

## Recommendations for the engine (not done here — engine modules were not touched)
1. `Traffic`: a per-class on/off that survives `Quality._rung` → `traffic.setFraction` (the game wraps the method on the instance), and a public hold / release for a mover (the game sets `m.base = 0`).
2. `DetailLayer.update(x, z, dist)`: gates on `dist < 85` and repaints a fixed 110 m window every 22 m; a street-level game needs a configurable window / look-ahead and an incremental repaint (paint a few rows per frame).
3. `Lighting.update` ties fog to the camera distance (`near = dist·1.9`): expose fog near / far (the game overwrites them every frame).
4. `ThemeManager`: a Game-free mode (or export `build(id)` + `_ground()`); `env.js` copies the three looks and the shader chain.
5. `World`: a constructor option "no hole game" (spawn, placement checks, mover registration), a public `hide(obj)` instead of the private `_hide`, and a way to set instance colours of a kind (the game lightens cars through `mesh.setColorAt`).
6. `PROP_DEFS.tram`: ship an articulated model (this game's `tramModel.js` could replace it); person variants with animation hooks.
7. `vite.config.js` `dataPreload`: a per-page flag instead of the path guard added for `/tramvajs/`.
8. `maps`: an `applyMap(slug)` that does not read the URL / localStorage.
9. Low tier on phones still draws ~1 M triangles: a street-level tree budget would help most.

## "Kur es esmu?" removal
`kur/`, `src/proto/kur/**`, `scripts/smoke-kur.mjs`, `docs/proto/kur.md`, the `kur` Vite input and the `smoke:kur` script are gone. `src/proto/shared/street-world.js` stays (the tram game needs it).
The host serves the main `index.html` for any unknown path (`not_found_handling: single-page-application`), all of its URLs are absolute (`/assets/…`, `/data/…`, `/sw.js`), so `/kur/` boots the main game; `vite.config.js`
now injects `<script>if (path is not "/" or "/index.html") location.replace('/' + search + hash)</script>` as the first thing in the main page's `<head>`, so the address bar ends on `/`.
Service worker: navigations are `fetch`ed first and only `/` / `/index.html` are ever put into the cache, so a `/kur/` page cannot be cached (checked: no `kur` entry in any cache after visiting it with the
worker active); **no `public/sw.js` change / cache bump was needed** (the offline navigation fallback serves the cached `/` shell, which is the main game).
